# Ordinary rollback and incident boundary

Stop peer/work generation first and request normal systemd stop. No automatic force-kill, forced module unload, reset/rebind or UMEM release. The unchanged FD supervisor can terminate tiles; process exit alone is not a proof of DMA quiescence.

Before ordinary restore require a matching clean terminal receipt, no owned AF_XDP sockets/XDP state, empty service and migrated cpuset, unchanged voter fence/management, no relevant firmware/driver fault including HWRM ring-free failure. HOLD does not authorize restoration. A transient post-exit cgroup observation in the completed trial was resolved by fresh complete checks; a reply timeout while restoring irqbalance was resolved by verifying original policy and completing the remaining restore steps. No mismatch was ignored. The current trial restored normally without reboot; the earlier missing-directory trial needed separately approved ordinary reboot recovery.

Restore saved stock driver/hash and all networkd/bond/address/MAC/routes/channel/ring/offload values. Restore IRQs by device/vector identity after module reload, not stale numeric IRQ number, and verify a single coherent final snapshot. Restore irqbalance/workqueue masks, loopback sysctls and console blink. Preserve firewall rules/counters. Only regenerated RSS keys on both data ports were accepted as an explicit exception in this experiment.

Remove only owner-verified workspace files after no mappings/process owners remain; unmount normally and restore original page counts. Remove only owned empty cpuset and transient units. Keep the original voter fence. Retire deadline only after a durable final stock-state verification.

Ambiguous DMA, kernel hang or unresponsive shutdown requires the site's separately approved console/ordinary-reboot incident plan. Timers cannot guarantee recovery from a hung kernel. Do not convert that limitation into permission for forced unload/power/reset actions.
